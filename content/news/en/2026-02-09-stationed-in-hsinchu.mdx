EZeeTech has been selected as a **2026 resident team at the Hsinchu Startup Hub**, marking a new stage in its technical validation and application development.

![Selection results for the Hsinchu Startup Hub’s 2026 residency](/news/a0b8c46d144c438a85f26f3d92007c5f.png)

The hardware development team previously won the national final of the **Hsinchu County National Student Entrepreneurship Challenge** at **Meet Taipei 2025**. Participation in that event helped the team build relationships with the Hsinchu area's entrepreneurship support network. The competition provided training, resources and industry connections as the team developed its vehicle vibration energy recovery proposal.

Through related events and discussions, EZeeTech also began exchanges with Mighty Net and other industry organizations about technology applications, industry needs and possible collaboration models. These conversations helped clarify the product's position in the regional supply chain and provided input for further development.

The team's research at the time focused on converting vehicle vibration into auxiliary electrical power. The proposed applications aim to improve energy utilization; field testing and operating data are important next steps in assessing feasibility and performance.

With the residency, EZeeTech plans to build on its validation work and advance toward field testing, connecting resources from industry, academia and the public sector. The hub provides workspace, mentoring, entrepreneurship training, investor introductions and industry connections.

Using Hsinchu as a development base, the team intends to explore applications across regions and industries and continue planning for commercialization and scaling. Selection for the residency is a milestone in the company's regional engagement and product development.
