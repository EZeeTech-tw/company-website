EZeeTech was selected in **March 2025** for the first 2025 residency at the **Taoyuan Youth Startup Hub**, established by the Taoyuan City Government's Department of Youth Affairs. The team was among the student startup teams selected for the program with a defined technology development direction.

![Selection announcement for the first 2025 residency at the Taoyuan Youth Startup Hub](/news/taoyuan-youth-command-114-announcement.png)

The selection recognized the team's technical feasibility, potential applications and plans for implementation. The residency provides workspace, mentoring and opportunities to connect across disciplines, supporting technical validation, pilot partnerships and business development.

The hub supports young entrepreneurs through individual mentoring, entrepreneurship training, investor introductions and industry connections. For EZeeTech, selection as a student team was an opportunity to advance its research into vehicle vibration energy recovery.

At the time of the announcement, EZeeTech was developing technology to convert mechanical vibration generated during vehicle operation into electrical energy. The research aims to improve energy utilization and explore auxiliary power applications in electric vehicles.

During the residency, the team plans to work with vehicle manufacturers, battery suppliers and other industry partners on validation and pilot projects. These activities would collect operating data, refine system performance and examine suitability across vehicle types and operating conditions. Participation in innovation competitions and industry exhibitions would also help the team gather feedback and build visibility.

EZeeTech will continue developing its technology and working with industry and public-sector partners to evaluate real-world applications in sustainable transportation and energy efficiency.
