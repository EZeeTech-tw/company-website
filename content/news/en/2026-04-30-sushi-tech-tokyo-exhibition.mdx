EZeeTech participated in **SusHi Tech Tokyo**, exhibiting in the Taiwan pavilion alongside other Taiwanese startup teams and sharing its work with an international audience.

On **April 27**, the team was invited to speak in a panel discussion at **Open Innovation Day**, sharing its entrepreneurial journey and plans with founders, investors and ecosystem representatives. The event was jointly organized by Meet Global and CDIB Capital. The discussions provided market feedback and new connections as the team explored opportunities in Japan's smart and sustainable technology markets.

The visit also included an exchange at **the University of Tokyo**, where the team discussed innovation, industry collaboration and research perspectives with local academics. It was an opportunity to connect the company's entrepreneurial work with research and future technology development. The itinerary also included visits to **Tokyo Innovation Base (TIB)** and **Startup Island TAIWAN Tokyo Hub**, two separate locations in Tokyo's startup ecosystem.
