On **October 29, 2025**, EZeeTech participated in the **2025 IAIC Smart Taoyuan industry-academia collaboration event** and received the **Rising Star Award**. The award recognized the team's technology direction, application potential and early development progress.

The event brought together startup teams and business representatives with extensive industry experience. For EZeeTech, a company in its first year of operation, exchanging ideas with more established teams and receiving recognition was both a milestone and a learning opportunity. The event was organized by Chung Yuan Christian University's Industry Accelerator and Incubation Center to promote collaboration between academia, startups and industry.

Other award recipients included PackAge+, Moldintel and teams working across manufacturing and other sectors. Sharing the stage with companies that had already raised funding and developed industry experience gave EZeeTech an opportunity to learn from different business and technology development paths.

At the time, EZeeTech's work focused on vehicle vibration energy recovery. The proposed approach converts mechanical vibration into auxiliary electrical power and explores potential applications in electric vehicles and sustainable transportation. The award reflected the team's early development potential.

EZeeTech thanks the organizers, judges, Chung Yuan Christian University's Industry Accelerator and Incubation Center, and the participating partners. The center supports teams through mentoring, entrepreneurship training, investor introductions and industry connections.

The team will continue its research, explore practical applications and partnerships, and develop its product and business model through technical validation and industry collaboration.
