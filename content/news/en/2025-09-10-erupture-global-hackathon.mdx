At a municipal meeting on **September 10, 2025**, Taoyuan Mayor Chang San-cheng recognized EZeeTech for taking **third place in the 2025 Erupture Global Hackathon**. The mayor highlighted the city's support for young entrepreneurs participating in international competitions and encouraged the team to continue developing innovations in smart transportation.

The Erupture competition held regional rounds in Asia, Europe and the Americas. Finalists traveled to the University of California, Berkeley for training and the global final. More than **300 teams worldwide**, including **120 teams from Taiwan**, entered the competition. EZeeTech advanced through the selection rounds and finished third globally.

EZeeTech's CEO thanked the city's Department of Youth Affairs and the Taoyuan City Government Youth Entrepreneurship Command Center for helping the team participate. The project presented at the competition focused on a vehicle piezoelectric energy-harvesting shock absorber and intelligent control. Its research aims include recovering vibration energy, improving energy utilization and investigating effects on driving comfort.

The Department of Youth Affairs noted that EZeeTech originated as a student startup team at Chung Yuan Christian University and received support through the city's startup hub. The hub provides mentoring, training, investor introductions and industry connections to help teams develop their technology and businesses.

The team will continue its research and industry engagement, using the experience to advance technical validation and commercialization planning.
