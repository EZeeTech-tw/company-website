On **November 21, 2025**, EZeeTech won the national championship in the **Youth Entrepreneurship Challenge**, jointly organized by the Hsinchu County Government and Business Next. Presenting its vehicle vibration energy recovery proposal, the team progressed through preliminary selection, semifinals and an entrepreneurship boot camp before competing in the final on the **Meet Taipei AI Stage**.

The competition focused on AI and technology-based solutions to real problems. EZeeTech's proposal examines how energy from vehicle vibration could be recovered without compromising safety and comfort. Converting that mechanical energy into auxiliary electrical power is intended to improve energy utilization and explore a contribution to electric-vehicle range.

Through the preliminary and semifinal rounds, the team refined its business model, clarified its technology roadmap and practiced communicating its proposal. Preparation for the final also strengthened planning for system integration and future market development. The judges assessed the proposal's innovation, market potential and commercial feasibility.

The challenge supports student entrepreneurship with training, resources and industry connections. Competing against teams from across Taiwan gave EZeeTech an opportunity to test its product positioning and learn from other founders and mentors.

**Further reading:** [Business Next's feature on the Hsinchu student entrepreneurship challenge](https://www.bnext.com.tw/article/85353/hsinchu.student).

The championship is a milestone in the team's development. EZeeTech will continue working with industry and public-sector partners to validate the technology, investigate practical applications and develop its business.
