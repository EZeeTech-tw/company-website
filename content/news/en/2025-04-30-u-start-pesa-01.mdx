On **April 30, 2025**, EZeeTech's **PESA-01 vehicle battery vibration energy recovery project** passed the review for the U-start Plan for Innovation and Entrepreneurship and was selected for funding.

![First-stage funding selection announcement for the 2025 U-start Plan for Innovation and Entrepreneurship](/news/u-start-114-pesa-01-announcement.png)

The review recognized the project's technical feasibility, defined application scenarios and plans for practical validation.

U-start is an entrepreneurship support program run by the Youth Development Administration of Taiwan's Ministry of Education. It helps young teams develop innovative ideas into business proposals, providing review and resources for technology development, market validation and business model development.

PESA-01 focuses on vibration energy recovery in vehicle battery-related applications. Its objective is to assess performance, stability and integration feasibility in vehicles and other mobile platforms, using pilot testing and data collection to move from proof of concept toward a deployable system. The proposed system converts vehicle vibration into electrical energy for auxiliary charging, with the aim of improving energy utilization.

With U-start support, EZeeTech plans to strengthen its validation process and begin application-oriented pilot tests. The team intends to work with vehicle manufacturers, battery suppliers and industry partners to collect operating data, optimize the system and assess its suitability across different vehicles and use cases.

The selection is a milestone in the team's development. Technical validation, system integration and pilot applications will remain the project's priorities, alongside industry engagement and participation in innovation competitions and exhibitions.
