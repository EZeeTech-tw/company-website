On **June 4, 2026**, EZeeTech was selected for a **startup subsidy under Taipei's Subsidies & Incentives for Taipei Industry (SITI) program**, supporting its development in energy recovery technology.

Administered by the Taipei City Government's Department of Economic Development, SITI offers support for startup development, research and development, branding and incubation. EZeeTech's selection recognized the proposal's technical innovation and potential applications in vehicle vibration energy recovery.

The team's application addressed three development priorities:

- **Technical innovation:** the design of vehicle vibration energy recovery technology.
- **Market potential:** possible applications in electric vehicles and sustainable transportation.
- **Commercial feasibility:** planning for technology commercialization and market development.

At the time of the announcement, EZeeTech was researching ways to convert mechanical vibration during vehicle operation into electrical energy for auxiliary charging. The research aims to improve energy utilization and explore a contribution to vehicle range.

With the subsidy's support, EZeeTech plans to continue technology development, strengthen collaboration and explore market opportunities. The team will work with industry and public-sector partners to advance validation and evaluate practical applications in sustainable transportation and energy efficiency.
