The official EZeeTech website is now live, providing structured information about the company's technology direction, collaboration models and latest developments.

The website is intended as a reference point for:

- Public- and private-sector partners.
- Program and grant reviewers.
- Media representatives and general inquiries.

At the time of the launch, EZeeTech's research focused on vehicle vibration energy recovery. The proposed technology converts mechanical vibration into electrical energy for auxiliary power applications, with the aim of improving energy utilization in electric vehicles and sustainable transportation.

The site brings together information on technology applications, product proposals, collaboration approaches and contact details. A clear information structure is intended to help potential partners understand the company's work and begin relevant technical or commercial discussions.

EZeeTech plans to share project milestones, pilot progress and partnership updates through this platform as its work develops. Research, technical validation and cooperation with industry and public-sector organizations will remain central to that progress.
