The regional final took place on **June 27, 2025**, at the **Taipei Innovation Lab**. It brought together **29 startup teams** from Taiwan, Hong Kong, Vietnam, Malaysia, Eswatini and Poland, competing with technology and innovation proposals.

EZeeTech presented its vehicle battery vibration energy recovery technology and business model. The proposed technology converts vehicle vibration into electrical energy for auxiliary charging, with the aim of improving energy utilization in electric vehicles. The team's presentation addressed technical innovation, market potential and commercial feasibility.

Following the regional result, EZeeTech will represent Asia at the global finals scheduled for **July 21–25, 2025**, at the **University of California, Berkeley**. The event brings regional finalists together for international exposure, investor engagement and industry connections. The team is preparing its pitch and technical presentation for that stage.

The result marks a milestone in EZeeTech's international participation. The team thanks the Taoyuan City Government's Department of Youth Affairs and the Youth Startup Hub for their support and mentoring, and will continue developing the technology and exploring practical applications in sustainable transportation.
