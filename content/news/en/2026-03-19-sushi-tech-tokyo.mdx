EZeeTech has been selected among **Taiwan's top 15 representative teams** for the **2026 Tokyo startup program**, supported by the Ministry of Economic Affairs' Small and Medium Enterprise and Startup Administration. The team will travel to Tokyo to participate in **SusHi Tech Tokyo 2026**.

Promoted by Startup Terrace and implemented by the Taipei Computer Association, the program helps selected startups build international market connections through exhibitions, business matching and local engagement. It is intended to support expansion in Asian markets and stronger relationships with Japan's innovation ecosystem.

SusHi Tech Tokyo brings together startups, corporate decision-makers and investors working in areas including AI, smart cities, sustainability and advanced technology applications. EZeeTech plans to present its work, exchange technical perspectives and meet potential partners and investors through business-matching sessions and on-site discussions. The team intends to use the visit to examine international market requirements and improve its understanding of potential applications.

The trip also includes a planned visit to **the University of Tokyo** for discussions with researchers about technology trends and application scenarios. These exchanges are intended to inform future research and possible industry-academia collaboration.

The team also plans to visit both **Tokyo Innovation Base (TIB)** and **Startup Island TAIWAN Tokyo Hub** to connect with Japan's startup ecosystem. Discussions with local teams and industry representatives will help it understand market requirements, support mechanisms and opportunities for localization.

EZeeTech has participated in innovation competitions and entrepreneurship programs in Taiwan and abroad to develop its experience and international perspective. Selection for this program is a step toward broader market engagement, with Taiwan remaining the company's base as it explores opportunities in Asia and beyond.

By participating in the delegation, the team aims to share Taiwanese technology with international audiences, learn from potential partners and bring those insights back into its product development.
