EZeeTech won the **startup category at the 21st Varsity Startup Strategy Competition**, presenting its vehicle vibration energy recovery proposal.

The final brought together **29 teams** competing for a total prize pool of **NT$320,000**. The team's presentation focused on its technology design, potential applications and plans for commercialization. The competition supports entrepreneurial teams through training, resources and industry connections.

A representative of the judging panel noted the participating teams' progress in technology development and market applications. EZeeTech had received support through the Ministry of Education's **U-start Plan for Innovation and Entrepreneurship**, which helped the team develop its proposal and business planning.

At the event, New Taipei City's deputy mayor called for the public sector to provide more resources and opportunities for startups. The head of the Ministry of Education's Youth Development Administration also encouraged teams to make use of U-start, which supports technology development, market validation and business model development.

EZeeTech's project investigates the recovery of mechanical vibration generated during vehicle operation and its conversion into auxiliary electrical power. The intended applications focus on energy efficiency and sustainable transportation. Further technical validation and collaboration are part of the team's plans for advancing the proposal toward practical use.

The award is a milestone in the company's development. EZeeTech will continue research, industry engagement and international collaboration while refining its commercialization and market plans.
