EZeeTech will exhibit at **COMPUTEX InnoVEX 2026** as part of the Taoyuan pavilion, presenting its work on smart energy recovery and sustainable transportation.

The team was selected by the **Taoyuan City Government's Department of Youth Affairs** and **Plug and Play Taiwan** to join the **TAOYUAN STARTUP × PLUG AND PLAY** pavilion.

The exhibition will provide an opportunity to share the team's research into recovering mechanical energy from vehicle operation and discuss applications for future transportation systems.
