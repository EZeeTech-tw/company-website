**On November 10, 2026, EZeeTech announced Composita OS, released its product introduction video and opened an early access waitlist.** Composita OS is an AI agent platform under development within Composita, focused on composite engineering analysis. Its goal is to reduce repetitive work in data preparation, layup definition and the handoff between analysis stages.

## Connecting the composite analysis workflow

Composite analysis brings together part geometry, material properties, fiber directions, layup configurations, loads and boundary conditions. Composita OS is being developed to connect these interdependent engineering settings through an agent workflow, helping engineers trace inputs, review the process and organize results.

Initial product discussions and early access planning focus on:

- **CAD and geometry preparation:** organizing component data and analysis prerequisites.
- **Material and layup modeling:** defining composite materials, ply sequences and fiber orientations.
- **Simulation workflow integration:** connecting the model, loads, boundary conditions and analysis settings.
- **Results and failure assessment review:** organizing analysis results, assessment settings and information for subsequent design changes.
