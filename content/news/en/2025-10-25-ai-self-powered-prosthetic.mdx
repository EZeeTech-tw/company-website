EZeeTech and AI health technology team NOVATERA have launched an **SDGs collaboration and AI motion-sensing integration project**. The collaboration explores an **AI Self-Powered Smart Prosthetic Platform**, combining vibration energy recovery with AI-assisted motion sensing.

EZeeTech's research into vibration energy recovery and shock absorption examines how mechanical movement can be converted into auxiliary electrical power. Building on work in electric vehicles and smart transportation, the collaboration proposes applying this approach to walking and joint motion, with the goal of supplying auxiliary energy to prosthetic sensors, processing electronics and communications modules.

## Motion sensing and analysis

NOVATERA's proposed AI health system brings together motion-sensing technologies and models of joint movement. The sensing approach includes:

- Three-axis acceleration.
- Three-axis angular velocity.
- Rotational motion and orientation estimation.
- Changes in body orientation and posture.

Inertial measurement units (IMUs) provide motion data for analyzing movement trajectories and posture. The proposed workflow connects **motion sensing → AI pose recognition → joint-angle calculation → movement-model analysis → prosthetic joint control**. The project describes this direction as AI biomechanical motion control.

## Energy recovery and joint control

The proposed energy-harvesting shock joint would absorb walking impacts and recover part of the mechanical energy as electricity. A control loop linking **sensors → edge AI processing → joint control → motion adjustment** is intended to investigate how prosthetic motion could respond to ground impact and changes in posture.

## Proposed health-platform functions

The collaboration also explores integration with a health data platform, including:

- Body pose detection.
- Range-of-motion (ROM) analysis.
- Gait analysis.
- Rehabilitation movement assessment.
- Posture-related feedback.
- Health data management.

These functions are proposed development directions for monitoring movement and supporting future rehabilitation workflows.

## Sustainable development goals

The project aligns its research direction with **SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being**, **SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure**, **SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities**, and **SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals**.

EZeeTech and NOVATERA plan to continue integrating AI motion sensing, movement-based energy recovery and health-platform research, exploring applications in smart prosthetics and assistive technology.
