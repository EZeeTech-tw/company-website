EZeeTech has passed the review for **Hon Hai Precision Industry's Patent Incubation Program** and has been selected as a patent collaboration team. The selection recognizes the company's work in vibration energy recovery and its intellectual property development plans.

For startups, patents can help protect research outcomes and support long-term technology development. Through the program, EZeeTech plans to strengthen its intellectual property portfolio, advance product validation and explore transportation and industrial applications for its research.

EZeeTech thanks Hon Hai's central intellectual property office for its recognition and support, as well as the team members, advisers and partners involved in the company's technology and intellectual property work.

The research described in this announcement focuses on converting mechanical vibration generated during vehicle operation into electrical energy for auxiliary charging. Improving energy utilization in vehicle battery systems is a development objective. Participation in the patent program is intended to support the protection and commercialization planning of this technology.

The company will continue technology development and industry collaboration, protecting research outcomes through its patent strategy and exploring opportunities to work with additional partners.
