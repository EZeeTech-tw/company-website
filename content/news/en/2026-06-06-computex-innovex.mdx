EZeeTech participated in **COMPUTEX and InnoVEX**, sharing its work with the international technology and startup community in Taipei.

The team thanks the **Taoyuan City Government** and **Plug and Play Tech Center** for creating the platform that supported its participation and helped connect Taiwanese startups with global audiences.

During the exhibition, the team exchanged ideas with founders and executives from the **United States, Europe, Israel, Japan and South Korea** working in sustainability, AI, hardware innovation and deep technology. These conversations highlighted the role of collaboration in turning research and technology into practical applications.

COMPUTEX and InnoVEX bring together technology companies, startups, investors and industry specialists, creating opportunities for technical demonstrations, business matching and industry engagement.

The connections made during the event will inform further discussions and possible partnerships. EZeeTech's research at the time focused on converting vehicle vibration into auxiliary electrical power, with the aim of improving energy utilization in electric vehicles and sustainable transportation.

The team welcomes further conversations with exhibition participants and people working in sustainable transportation, clean technology and energy innovation. Through technical development and collaboration with industry and public-sector partners, EZeeTech will continue evaluating real-world applications and developing its commercialization plans.
